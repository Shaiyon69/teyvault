Built to be safe

Your account stays yours

Teyvault only does what the official HoYoLAB pages do, at the same pace.

⚿

Login in your OS keyring

Your HoYoLAB cookies go in Windows Credential Manager, or the app's private storage on mobile. Never in the database, never shared.

✓

Official endpoints only

Only the requests hoyolab.com and the in-game wish page make. No reading game memory, no injection.

◔

Polite by design

Requests go one at a time and spaced out. One check-in a day, with a cooldown between codes.

⌂

Local first

Your wish history stays in a database on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

FAQ

Questions

Can this get my account banned?

Teyvault never touches the game client. It only reads the wish link the game already caches, and calls the same HoYoLAB endpoints your browser does, spaced out like a person would.

Which servers does it support?

All global servers: America, Europe, Asia and TW/HK/MO. Chinese mainland servers use a different platform and aren't supported.

How do I get my wish history on Android?

Open the wish history in-game on your PC, sync in the Windows app, then use Phone sync. Or import a UIGF or paimon.moe Excel export on the phone.

Can I bring my history from paimon.moe or other trackers?

Yes. Teyvault imports UIGF v2–v4 files and paimon.moe Excel exports, and replaces imported rows with the real ones once you sync.

Why does sign-in ask for a captcha?

HoYoLAB asks for a Geetest check on most sign-ins. Teyvault shows the same check the website does.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no premium tier. The source is on GitHub.
